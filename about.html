About

01 / How I work How I think

Greener taught me that restraint is not the same as taking everything out. I removed the illustrated map and the cartoon styling, because they were there to impress rather than to help. With the location check I did the opposite. It used to run silently, and I gave it its own card so people could watch it happen, because that was the part they needed to trust.

So what I believe for now is that the work is deciding what deserves to be seen, and staying quiet about everything else.
